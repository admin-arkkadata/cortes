Cortes Publicador

Cortes Publicador is a private tool used only by the owner of two YouTube channels to upload short highlight clips (Shorts) from livestreams to those same channels:

  • Cortes na Vice (Brazilian Portuguese)
  • Vice Sirens (English)

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account.
