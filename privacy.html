Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Cortes Publicador ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels Cortes na Vice and Vice Sirens. It is used only by that owner.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their title, description and privacy status on the owner's own channels. It requests only the permission needed to upload videos (youtube.upload).

What data is collected

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. The only credential it keeps is the owner's own authorization token, stored locally on the owner's computer and never shared with third parties.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
