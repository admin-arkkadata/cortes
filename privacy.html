Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Cortes Publicador ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the channels Cortes na Vice and Vice Sirens (YouTube, TikTok and Instagram). It is used only by that owner.

What the app accesses

The app uses the official YouTube, TikTok and Instagram APIs only to upload videos, with their title or caption, to the owner's own accounts, and to read the basic profile of those accounts to confirm where it is posting.

What data is collected

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. The only credential it keeps is the owner's own authorization token, stored locally on the owner's computer and never shared with third parties.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
